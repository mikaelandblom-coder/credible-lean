corrBS (the default reference): Bühlmann–Straub with one correlation ρ̂ and region variances shrunk towards the pooled one by their own credibility; every parameter by a moment equation, no damping, nothing to tune, K̂ ≻ 0 by a theorem. “BLUP” plugs corrBS’s K̂ into the best linear unbiased predictor, which uses every other region individually instead of only through μ̂. “corr” uses the thesis estimators (37)–(41) with a damped K̂. “common” (one covariance for all pairs) is not recommended: with unequal volumes its z explode.
